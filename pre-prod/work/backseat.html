The problem

Wellington commuters had no quick way to share, or check, what a bus or train was actually like. Crowding and delays went unreported.

What I did

Designed a crowdsourced vibe and report app with five house rules, then rebuilt it as a live bus map using the Metlink API.

The result

Live at thebackseat.co.nz. [ONE LINE ON WHAT THE NUMBERS SAY]
