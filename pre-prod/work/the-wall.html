The problem

How might we build a community platform that encourages meaningful sharing, without the overwhelming complexity of traditional social media?

The goal was to test whether simplicity and constraints could lead to better quality interactions.

The idea

Projects as the unit of conversation. No endless feed. Each project becomes a wall where people post updates, questions or thoughts.

That constraint was the point: centring everything on a project keeps discussion focused, not random social chatter.

What I did

Every decision aimed to cut friction and keep things clear:

  • Everything lives inside a project
  • Posts are a title plus a question
  • A clean, distraction-free reading view
  • Colour only for state and status, never decoration

Result

Early testers appreciated the simplicity but needed more context about what each project was for. Adding project descriptions and clear status indicators helped people see where to contribute.

[ANY NUMBERS FROM TESTING?]

What I learned

The hardest part is knowing what to leave out. Constraints can be freeing, for designers and users. Limiting what's possible made what's left more intentional.
